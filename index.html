Vista · San Diego

I got into this because of records — the ones that feel alive, that sound like a room, like something real happened. Trained in Nashville. Moved back to California. Spent twenty years in studios chasing that feeling on every project that comes through the door.

Indie rock is my home. All the directions it goes — loud and abrasive, quiet and strange, direct and anthemic. I'll meet your music where it is.

My clients say my mixes are dynamic, distinctive, and capturing. I think that just means I listen.
