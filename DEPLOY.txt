# Deploy samsterkaudio.com to Cloudflare Pages
# Quick start — 15 minutes, free hosting, automatic HTTPS

## What you need
- A free Cloudflare account (cloudflare.com)
- Your domain (samsterkaudio.com) — either already at Cloudflare or at any registrar

---

## STEP 1 — Upload your site files

1. Log in at dash.cloudflare.com
2. Click "Pages" in the left sidebar
3. Click "Create a project" → "Upload assets"
4. Name your project: samsterkaudio
5. Drag and drop the entire "samsterkaudio" folder
   (includes: index.html, _redirects, _headers)
6. Click "Deploy site"

Cloudflare gives you a free URL instantly:
  https://samsterkaudio.pages.dev

---

## STEP 2 — Connect your custom domain

### If samsterkaudio.com is already at Cloudflare:
1. In your Pages project → "Custom domains" tab
2. Click "Set up a custom domain"
3. Enter: samsterkaudio.com
4. Click "Continue" — Cloudflare sets up DNS automatically
5. Also add: www.samsterkaudio.com (redirects to apex via _redirects file)
6. SSL certificate provisions in ~2 minutes

### If samsterkaudio.com is at another registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.):

Option A — Move DNS to Cloudflare (recommended, free):
1. In Cloudflare dashboard → "Add a site" → enter samsterkaudio.com
2. Select Free plan
3. Cloudflare shows you 2 nameservers, e.g.:
     ns1.cloudflare.com
     ns2.cloudflare.com
4. Log in to your registrar → find "Nameservers" or "DNS" settings
5. Replace existing nameservers with Cloudflare's two
6. Wait 10–30 min for propagation
7. Back in Pages → "Custom domains" → add samsterkaudio.com

Option B — Point DNS records directly (stays at current registrar):
1. In your Pages project → "Custom domains" → add samsterkaudio.com
2. Cloudflare shows you a CNAME or A record to add
3. Log in to your registrar's DNS panel
4. Add that record
5. Wait up to 24hr for propagation

---

## STEP 3 — Future updates

When you want to update the site:
1. Edit index.html on your computer
2. Go to your Pages project → "Deployments"
3. Click "Upload assets" again
4. Drag the updated files
5. New version goes live instantly
